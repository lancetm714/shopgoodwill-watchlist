Telegram notifications

Backend checks every poll interval and refreshes the countdown in the message every countdown update seconds (faster in the final minutes). Sends once per item within its alert window, using only end times you entered — never ShopGoodwill's site or API.

How to get your token & chat ID

  1. In Telegram, message @BotFather and send /newbot. Follow the prompts to name your bot — it returns a bot token like 123456789:AAbb....
  2. Message your new bot once (any message).
  3. Open https://api.telegram.org/bot<TOKEN>/getUpdates in a browser and look for "chat":{"id":... — that number is your chat ID.
  4. Paste both above and click Save, then Send test notification.
